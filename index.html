FAQ

Good to know.

Is my reservation confirmed when I email?

No. Your request must be approved first. I'll contact you after reviewing it.

How far ahead can I request?

Requests can be made 2 days in advance.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel more than 24 hours before your rental for a refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are charged.

What if the weather is bad?

If bad weather makes the session unsafe, you'll receive a refund.

Do I need to know how to use the machine?

No. I'll meet you about 10 minutes before the session and show you how it works.

How do I pay?

Payment is made in person after your reservation is approved.
